Beyond COMEX: who else reports the metal

Every COMEX number on VaultPulse starts with COMEX: the vaults report their own counts to CME, and CME publishes them. Cross-checking republished copies only shows the copies agree. This page adds holdings published by organisations COMEX doesn't control: the London Bullion Market Association, two Shanghai exchanges, and the largest silver and gold trusts, plus the retail premiums people actually pay. Each figure shows its own source, link and date, because they are published on different schedules.

Data checked 2026-11-12 15:02 UTC. Holdings are not added together: the same bar can appear in more than one row (for example, SLV's and GLD's London bars are also inside the LBMA London total).

What each source can and can't prove

  • COMEX (CME): daily and detailed by vault, but the depositories report their own counts, and it doesn't say who owns the metal or whether eligible metal is for sale.
  • LBMA London vaults: covers every major London vault, including the Bank of England's gold. It is monthly, published about five business days after month end, and is an aggregate of what the vaults report. It includes metal already counted by London-vaulted ETFs.
  • Shanghai (SHFE, SGE): independent of CME and frequent (SHFE daily, SGE weekly), but they are exchange-reported, and SHFE counts only warranted metal, so it is closer to COMEX registered than to COMEX total.
  • ETF bar lists (SLV, GLD, PSLV): the most granular public record, bar by bar, with periodic counts by outside inspectors. They are still published by the trusts and their custodians, and they show only the trust's own metal.
  • Premiums: real prices paid at dealers, so the hardest to fake, but they reflect retail coins and bars, not wholesale vaults.
  • None of these is an independent, bar-by-bar public audit of all the metal. Every source is self-reported to some degree.

FAQ

Why look beyond COMEX?

COMEX inventory figures come from the depositories themselves via CME. London, Shanghai and ETF data are published by other organisations, so they give an independent view of how much metal is reported in other vaults.

Can I add these holdings together?

No. The same bar can appear in more than one source; for example, SLV and GLD bars held in London are also inside the LBMA London vault total.

Which source is the most detailed?

The ETF bar lists. SLV, GLD and PSLV publish every bar they hold, with serial number, refiner and weight.
