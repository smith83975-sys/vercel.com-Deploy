Physical tightness score

How the score is calculated

Score (0–100, higher = tighter) = average of two components:

  1. Inventory component = 100 − percentile of today's registered ounces within the recent daily history we hold (currently ~30 days for silver).
  2. Coverage component = 100 − min(100, registered oz ÷ (futures open interest × contract size) × 100).

If a component's input is unavailable it is shown as pending and the score uses the remaining component only (marked "partial"). The short history window means the percentile is a recent-trend measure, not a long-term one.

What is force majeure?

Force majeure is a contract clause that lets a party delay or skip its obligations when extraordinary events outside its control happen, such as war, a natural disaster, or a government order. In a COMEX context, it could allow delays to physical delivery during a genuine emergency instead of counting as a breach. This is general information only. It is not a prediction that it will happen, and it is not financial advice.
