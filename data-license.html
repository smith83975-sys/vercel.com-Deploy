How to credit us

A short line is enough, ideally with a link and the data date, for example:

Underlying source data

Our figures are built from public data published by others. That underlying source data remains the property of its original publishers and is subject to their own terms of use, not this license. These include:

  • CME Group (COMEX warehouse stocks, delivery notices, daily bulletin open interest)
  • US Treasury and FiscalData (gold reserve, Debt to the Penny)
  • FRED / Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (M1, M2, commodity prices)
  • EIA, USGS, US DOE, BEA and the Congressional Research Service (resource estimates)
  • Bullion dealers and price-comparison sites (retail premiums)
  • The Vault Report, MetalCharts and other aggregators, gold-api.com (spot prices), and news publishers

If you need the raw source data, get it from the original publisher and follow their terms. Each VaultPulse page lists its sources and data dates.
